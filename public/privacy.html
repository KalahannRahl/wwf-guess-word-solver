Privacy Policy

Guess Word Solver — Words With Friends Helper

Short version: Guess Word Solver does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It is a simple puzzle helper tool — you type a score and it suggests words.

What This App Does

Guess Word Solver is a puzzle-helper tool for the Words With Friends daily Guess Word puzzle. You enter a target score and optional letter hints; the app searches a local word database and returns a list of matching words.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration or sign-in is required
  • No names, email addresses, or contact information are collected
  • No location data is collected
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs are used
  • No usage analytics or tracking are performed

How the App Works

All word-solving logic runs on our server. When you press Solve, your score and letter filters are sent as a simple request to our server, which returns matching words. No personal data is included in these requests — only the puzzle parameters you type in (a score number and optional letter positions).

The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and caches a small set of static files on your device for offline loading. This cache contains only app shell files (HTML, CSS, icons) and no personal data.

Third-Party Services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This is a standard web service; Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy. No other third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks are used.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users. There is no user data collection of any kind.
