## Full Description
*(4000 characters max)*

```
Stuck on today's Words With Friends Guess Word puzzle? This solver does the math for you.

Enter the target score from your puzzle board and the app instantly finds every valid 5-letter word that can hit that score — filtered to your exact bonus tile positions and letter clues.

HOW IT WORKS

Every Guess Word puzzle is placed on a Triple Word row with bonus tiles at specific positions. The scoring formula is always the same:

  (Highest letter × 3 + 2nd highest × 2 + remaining letters) × 3

Enter your target score, tap the positions where the board's bonus tiles are, and hit SOLVE. The app cross-references a full dictionary and returns only words that match your exact score and bonus layout.

FEATURES

• Score solver — enter any target score (must be divisible by 3) and get matching words instantly
• Bonus position lock — tap 1–3 positions to mark where your top letters must land; eliminates the wrong words before you even guess
• Guess tracker — enter your previous guesses and tap each letter tile green (right spot), yellow (wrong spot), or plain (not in the word) to narrow the list after each round
• Color-coded keyboard — letters grouped by point value so you always know which letters score highest
• Smart starters — suggests the best opening words calculated from your specific candidate list
• Excludes plurals — -S endings are hidden by default since puzzle answers are almost never regular plurals
• Works offline — Progressive Web App, works without internet after first load

LETTER VALUES (Words With Friends)

1pt: T S R O I E A
2pt: U N L D
3pt: Y H G
4pt: W P M F C B
5pt: K V
8pt: X
10pt: Q J Z

This is an unofficial helper tool and is not affiliated with Words With Friends or Zynga.
```
